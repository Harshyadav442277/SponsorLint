# Third-party notices

## Arlan Vault motion experiments

Parts of SponsorLint's browser motion system are adapted from the following
Arlan Vault experiments:

- Fade Motion: https://www.arlan.me/vault/fade-motion
- Dia Browser's Gradient: https://www.arlan.me/vault/dia-gradient
- Chromatic Glow: https://www.arlan.me/vault/chroma-glow
- Kinetic Typography: https://www.arlan.me/vault/kinetic-typography

The Vault declares its experiments “MIT — free to copy.” SponsorLint ports the
relevant rendering and lifecycle techniques from React/TypeScript/WebGL into
framework-free JavaScript and uses its own copy, typography, and color palette.
